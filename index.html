A little about me

Worlds are built
one choice at a time.

Hey, I'm Laurel Adiukwu, a narrative designer and fiction writer interested in the places where story, character and player choices meet. My work spans branching interactive fiction, RPG dialogue, high fantasy worldbuilding and episodic storytelling.

I enjoy creating characters with lives and motivations beyond the immediate scene, building worlds with their own histories and rules, and shaping choices, and plot events that feel consequential without losing sight of the human story at their centre.
